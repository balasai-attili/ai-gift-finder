Why we built GiftGenius AI

Choosing a gift is hard. You either spend hours scrolling through options, or you default to something generic. We believe every person deserves a gift that shows you truly thought about them.

GiftGenius AI combines the power of large language models with a curated dataset of gifting knowledge — trained on thousands of occasion-interest-gift pairings — so every suggestion feels personal and meaningful.

  • Trained on 1,000+ curated gift data examples across 15+ occasions
  • Context-aware suggestions that match personality and interests
  • Budget-conscious recommendations in Indian Rupees
  • Designed for Indian festivals, relationships, and cultural context
  • Continuously improving with user feedback
